## Data integrity, cold/warm transfer and large-scale E2E

- **EPIC9:** historical 10/10 PASS original report with 100,000 activation events and 200 positive stages; separate local cold runs of 10,000 activations; conditions and payload are separately accounted for.
- **1-bit and 1-byte activation:** 10,000 historical cold activations PASS, four invalid triggers rejected in a recorded run. A logical trigger is not necessarily the entire transmitted data.
- **9-bit / approximately 1.1-byte tests:** positive catalog/blueprint cases and multi-stage tests; 9 bits = 1.125 bytes. Receiver prerequisites and additional payload remain explicit.
- **0.01-bit probe:** requested historical label, original unambiguous successful physical digital transmission report not yet linked. Not claimed as verified PASS.
- **4 GiB reconstruction:** 2/2 historical PASS, 4,294,967,296 bytes, 31.76 and 33.39 seconds reported, no divergent blocks.
- **100 GiB scale/recovery:** 107,374,182,400 bytes source, 14,476,957,280 bytes reported wire and two successful restore checks.
- **WAN TRUE-COLD, Verify-Relay, 64 MiB blackbox, 128 MiB Golden roundtrip, 1 GiB external matrix proof, enwik9 single-package, TCP failover, local restore, replay and integrity families:** separate historically recorded scenarios. **The exact number of unique independent E2E scenarios, including the suggested ~50, is not yet established.**
- **enwik9 local research case:** 1,000,000,000 source bytes to 323,086,738 bytes; SHA-identical restoration reported; separate 323,086,689-byte case recorded under a different measurement scope.
- **Matrix data path:** historical cold reduction 81.2486%, warm 99.9916%, delta 99.9541% in specified reference runs.
