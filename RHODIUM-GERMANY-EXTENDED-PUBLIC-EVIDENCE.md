**Measurement boundary:** a logical 1-bit trigger is not necessarily a 1-bit total transmitted package. Nine bits equal 1.125 bytes, often rounded to 1.1 bytes. Receiver prerequisites and any additional payload must be counted separately. A literal 0.01-bit transmission is not a standard independently addressable digital payload; the requested 0.01-bit probe is listed for traceability, **not asserted as independently proven PASS** without its exact original report. The historical 0.01-ms entry is also not a validated complete restore benchmark. No independent Android-mobile-network-to-Windows-Wi-Fi cold end-to-end PASS is claimed.
